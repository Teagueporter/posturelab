# Support

PostureLab is a posture self-tracking web app. It is not medical software and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or clinician review.

## Product Support

For product bugs, setup problems, or account questions, email `teagueporter5@gmail.com`.

Include:

- The page or workflow where the issue happened
- Browser and device
- Whether you were signed in
- Whether the issue involved local scans, cloud sync, billing, export, or deletion

Do not include secret keys, `.env.local`, raw payment details, or scan photos from someone else.

## Medical Boundary

For pain, numbness, weakness, injury, dizziness, breathing problems, or symptoms that worry you, stop using the app for guidance and talk to a licensed clinician. PostureLab can help track photo-based posture trends, but it cannot tell whether an exercise is safe for a specific medical condition.
